Warning: the privacy policy is all AI slop (but it is accurate).

Last updated November 16, 2026.

Starmodder is a list of Starsector mods, made by one person, Wisp. This page covers the Starmodder website and its Discord app, Starmodder Catalog. It says what they collect, what is published, how long things are kept, and how to opt out or have your data deleted.

What is collected

From the Starsector forum

Mod threads on the forum's mod boards and the mod index. For each thread: its title, the opening posts by the thread's author (up to ten), the author's forum name and avatar, the links and pictures in those posts, and their dates. The forum is public, and nothing is read that a visitor cannot see.

From Discord

Starmodder Catalog reads the mod announcement channels of the Unofficial Starsector Discord. For each thread in those channels it reads the thread's name and tags, up to 100 of its messages, and who reacted with 🕸️. It keeps only:

  • the thread's name, tags and link,
  • the opening messages written by the thread's author (up to ten): their text, the names and links of attached pictures and files, and when they were posted and edited,
  • the author's Discord username.

Replies by anyone else are not kept. The app does not read direct messages, other channels, member lists or anyone's online status.

From Nexus Mods

The public pages of Starsector mods: name, author, summary, description and pictures.

How it is used

  • To build the Starmodder website and the catalog that the TriOS mod manager reads.
  • An AI language model reads each post to pick out details such as the mod's version, downloads, required mods, save compatibility and changelog, and to write a short summary. This model runs on Wisp's own computer. Discord posts are not sent to any cloud AI service.
  • Posts are never used to train AI models.
  • Nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

What is published

Starmodder and the public StarsectorModRepo repository on GitHub, which TriOS reads, show:

  • From a forum thread: the mod's name, the author's name, the whole first post, its pictures and links, and the details the model picked out.
  • From a Discord thread: the mod's name, the author's Discord username, the first two lines of the post, short quotes about its versions, save compatibility, conflicts and AI use, links to its pictures and downloads, its dates, and a link back to the thread. The rest of the post's text is not published.

GitHub keeps a history of the repository, so older versions of these files stay there. Versions from before mid-November 2026 also include the full text of Discord posts.

How long it is kept

  • The saved copy of each Discord post is replaced at every update, twice a day. Only the latest copy is kept. A thread that is deleted, or that you opt out of, is dropped at the next update.
  • Channels for older game versions are not read at every update. Their saved copies stay until you ask for them to be deleted.
  • The details the model picked out of a post stay on Wisp's server until you ask for them to be deleted. Records of past updates are kept for about eight months.

Visiting the website

Starmodder has no accounts, cookies, analytics or ads. Your settings and favorites are saved in your own browser and are never sent anywhere. The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles every request and may keep its own logs under its privacy policy. Pictures and videos in mod posts load from the sites their authors put them on, and those sites see that request. "Report a problem" links go to GitHub.

Starmodder is not meant for anyone under 13.

Opting out and deleting your data

  • On Discord: react to your own mod post with 🕸️ (:spider_web:). At the next update the thread is left out of Starmodder and its saved text is dropped. Only a reaction from the post's author counts. For channels of older game versions, message Wisp instead.
  • Anywhere: message Wisp to have your mods removed from Starmodder, or to ask what is kept about you, have it corrected or have it deleted. Please message from the account you post your mods with.
