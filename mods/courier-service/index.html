Courier Service

This is a lightweight QoL mod that adds a new character, John Courier, to the comm directory on Eochu Bres. John Courier lets you doordash (almost) everything you need to build your fleet. Pick what you want, place an order, and your items will be sourced from markets across the sector and delivered to your personal storage. No more flying from system to system hoping the right weapon is in stock, provided you're willing to pay the steep markup.
