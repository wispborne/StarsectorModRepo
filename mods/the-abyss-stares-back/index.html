The Abyss Stares Back

Gives the shrouded eye a laser pointer that instills fear in any ship it touches, causing them to back away. This may make shrouded fights easier, depending on whether or not you are running ships that could otherwise just facetank the eye. Comes in two flavors, one with a visible targeting laser (I SEE YOU), one without
