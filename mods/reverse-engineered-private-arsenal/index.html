Reverse Engineered Private Arsenal

Ever wanted to copy that rare weapon or ship you found instead of hoping for a blueprint drop? Build a Reverse Engineering Hub on your colony. Deposit weapons, fighters, or ships into storage. Each day the hub researches one item. Once done, you get fifty copies to buy from an integrated Private Arsenal submarket at a configurable price. The hub comes in three tiers. Tier 1 handles weapons, Tier 2 adds fighters, Tier 3 adds ships (but you only get their blueprints when improved). Assign an AI core for bonuses: it speeds up research AND cuts the Arsenal prices.
