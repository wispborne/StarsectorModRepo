Arma Armatura

A story and content mod primarily focused around pilotable strikecraft: fighter-scale ships with own unique mechanics, over 30 pilotable units across 18 chassis families, 7 warships and carriers built to support them, and 9 fighter wings, a fully fleshed out companion officer, a minor faction with campaign layer elements, and a story centering around a system level conflict.
