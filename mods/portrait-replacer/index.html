Portrait Replacer

Small utility mod that replaces some modded portraits with vanilla ones. The mod was created by me because certain portrait styles took me out of the game. Fully extendable in data/config/modSettings.json. ATM the mod replaces the portraits with random ones from vanilla on every game launch, a lot of functionality can be customized in the settings. Can be added/removed any time without issues. For any issues please ping me. Should not crash your game, but I may have left some debug crashes in by accident
