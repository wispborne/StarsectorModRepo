Shadowyards Reconstruction Authority

The SRA is the most lasting incarnation of a long line of anti-Domain movements which survived by virtue of remaining one step ahead of the Domains authority intruding into newly colonized space. Rejecting the vast machines of the Domains' industry, this lineage of rebel groups had a noted history of modifying, and in some instances designing, machinery and arms to suit their own needs. In the decades leading up to the collapse this group came under the control of one Alexandr Inokkenti, who put it’s technical expertise to use creating warships and weapons with which to oppose the Domain. ## Mod created by MShadowy
