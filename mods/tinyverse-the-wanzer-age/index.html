Tinyverse - The Wanzer Age

With the introduction of Diable Avionics into the sector and their military successes thanks to reintroducing the long abandoned technology of Wanzers, giant piloted mechanized robots capable of fighting both in-planet and out in the vacuum of space, showcased that it was time to consider the era of dominance of the spaceship and fighters had come to an end. Factions all over the sector have scrambled to bring back old, forgotten relics out of the dusty hangars where they had been forgotten. Others have used their ample monetary resources to acquire Wanzer blueprints and schematics from both legal and more illicit means, and others have straight-up used their vast production powers to jumpstart their Wanzer development programs. A new arms race has begun.
