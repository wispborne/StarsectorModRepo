systemRescan

A Starsector mod by banano of doom. For Starsector 0.98a. Version 20260110.

somewhat of a spiritual successor of the old "object analysis" https://fractalsoftworks.com/forum/index.php?topic=20331.0 given that the original doesn't _quite_ work all that well and takes up an ability slot, i decided to just remake it from scratch as a sidebar plugin that way it always stays on screen and doesn't require on-hover action runs passively when enabled, rescans system every 10 seconds or when you enter new starsystem
